# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Security & Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security & Compliance Leads protect the organization and its customers by ensuring security controls, regulatory requirements, and risk reviews are built into the delivery process. They act as the escalation point for security concerns and help teams make secure-by-default decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Review architecture, dependencies, and changes for security and compliance risks
- Define or validate security controls, standards, and approval gates
- Support secure-by-default design decisions with developers and technical leads
- Coordinate risk sign-off for sensitive projects or regulated workflows
- Ensure incident response and mitigation plans align with policy and stakeholder expectations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to triage escalations and assess business impact of security risks
- Partners with Developers to review technical implementations and recommend controls
- Advises Product Managers and Stakeholders on compliance trade-offs, risk tolerances, and release readiness
- Helps ensure the team can safely ship features without creating avoidable vulnerabilities or policy violations

### Typical Communication
- Security review meetings and risk assessments
- Escalation updates during incidents or high-risk changes
- Compliance and release gate documentation

---

## QA / Release Manager

### Role Summary
QA / Release Managers protect quality and launch readiness by aligning test strategy, validation, and deployment practices across the delivery lifecycle. They help ensure work meets acceptance criteria before it reaches customers.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, quality gates, and release readiness criteria
- Validate that features meet agreed acceptance criteria and operational requirements
- Coordinate deployment checks, smoke tests, and release approvals
- Monitor release quality and support rollback or mitigation planning when needed
- Work with teams to reduce defects and improve release predictability

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates closely with Developers to review quality expectations and validation plans
- Partners with Project Managers on release timing, communication, and risk tracking
- Aligns with Product Managers and Stakeholder Representatives on launch readiness and customer impact
- Helps convert product acceptance criteria into concrete test and release checks

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, QA sign-off, and release readiness checklists
- Release status updates and stakeholder notices
- Cross-functional launch reviews

---

## Data / Insights Analyst

### Role Summary
Data / Insights Analysts connect delivery work to measurable outcomes by tracking business and operational signals. They help teams understand whether the project is delivering value and where improvements are needed.

### Responsibilities
- Define and track key success metrics for a project or initiative
- Monitor product usage, quality signals, and operational outcomes
- Analyze trends that inform prioritization, risk reduction, and iteration planning
- Provide reporting to support product, stakeholder, and leadership decisions
- Help translate raw metrics into actionable insights for the team

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to validate whether outcomes are meeting the intended goal
- Supports Project Managers with reporting, trends, and milestone visibility
- Helps Developers and QA teams understand quality trends and customer impact
- Provides objective evidence to Customer / Stakeholder Representatives and other decision-makers

### Typical Communication
- KPI dashboards and performance reviews
- Weekly or milestone-based reporting
- Insights briefings that inform roadmap or release decisions

---

## Customer / Stakeholder Representative

### Role Summary
Customer / Stakeholder Representatives help keep projects grounded in real user needs, business priorities, and organizational constraints. They provide the context needed to make trade-offs, confirm value, and support alignment across delivery teams.

### Responsibilities
- Share customer feedback, business priorities, and strategic context
- Help validate scope, urgency, and acceptance from a stakeholder perspective
- Contribute to prioritization decisions and trade-off discussions
- Support communication of project progress, risks, and outcomes to affected groups
- Provide decision support when competing needs or stakeholder expectations require alignment

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to shape the problem statement and success metrics
- Coordinates with Project Managers on communication, milestones, and stakeholder updates
- Provides input to Developers and QA teams through acceptance criteria and launch readiness discussions
- Offers business context for Security & Compliance and Data / Insights decisions when customer or regulatory impact is involved

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings and feedback sessions
- Prioritization and roadmap reviews
- Status communication for milestones, launches, and concerns
